# Review "a11y": accessibility auditor lens (WCAG 2.2 AA + Springboard rules)

Reviewer: a11y. Build under review: `/home/user/LG-Proto1/dist` (fresh, `app.js` 21:04). Browser: Chromium only (Playwright 1.56 via `tests/lib/harness.mjs`), real keyboard events, CDP accessibility tree.
Scratch (git-ignored): `.scratch/a11y/` (scripts, JSON, screenshots). New reusable test: `tests/review/a11y-checks.mjs` (32 checks: 24 encode a defect and fail today, 8 are regression guards and pass; exits 1).

## 1. Verdict

This is a strong accessibility baseline, better than most prototypes. axe-core is clean on every route and overlay in both themes at desktop and phone width (the only axe finding anywhere is the known chart-tooltip `region`). A pixel-backed contrast scan of 28,282 text runs, glyph icons, SVG labels and placeholders found no text below 4.5:1 and no overlay (dialogs, drawers, menu, toasts, tooltips) below AA. A real-keyboard run of the whole demo script works end to end: skip link, route-change focus on the h1, a visible focus ring on all 781 tab stops measured, every overlay traps Tab, closes on Escape and returns focus, a confirm over a drawer closes alone, reduced motion switches animation off.

What an auditor would still fail: (1) **reflow and zoom**: "Show table" on Cap vs spend pans the whole shell sideways (138px at 1024x768, 686px at 390px); Settings and Share vanish below 700px (that is 200% zoom on a 1366px laptop); the rail Menu is clipped at 200% zoom of a 1440x900 screen; (2) **target size**: the demo-critical "View clause, page N" link, "See calculation", "See payments" and every sortable column header are 16px tall, under the 24px Springboard minimum, and sit inside the stretched row hit area so the spacing exception does not rescue them; (3) a cluster of quieter name, state and forced-colours defects (icon glyphs leaking into accessible names, no `aria-expanded` on Menu, shell tooltips that cannot be dismissed, a filter-chip state that is hue only). No blocker: nothing stops the five-minute demo, and no primary flow is inaccessible by keyboard.

## 4. Findings (most important first)

Ids `a11y-NN` in the structured result are `A-NN` here. Each: area that owns the fix, where, evidence, expected, fix. Tests encode the defects as `Dxx` in `tests/review/a11y-checks.mjs`.

### Major

**A-01 (shared, major) "Show table" on Cap vs spend pans the whole shell sideways.** (D03)
Where: `#/spend`, "Show table" twin, `.kviz-tablewrap` in `src/charts/charts.css:113` (same flaw as `.kx-table-wrap` in `src/ui/kit.css`).
Evidence: `documentElement.scrollWidth - clientWidth` after pressing Show table = 138px at 1024x768, 393px at 683x384, 686px at 390x844, 756px at 320x568, 438px at 720x450, 564px at 512x384; 0 at 1366 and 1440. The twin on Opportunities, Renewals and Contract detail is fine (0 at every size). Cause: the `.sr-only` spans inside the table's links (`position: absolute`) have no positioned ancestor, so they escape the scroll wrapper. This is the accessible alternative to the chart, and 1024x768 is a stated demo size.
Expected: no horizontal page scroll (WCAG 1.4.10, R8).
Fix: `.kviz-tablewrap, .kx-table-wrap { position: relative }`. One line.

**A-02 (shared, major) Settings, Share and Notifications disappear below 700px, so Settings cannot be reached at 200% zoom on a 1366px laptop.** (D01)
Where: `src/shell/shell.css` `@media (max-width: 699px) { .shell__tab, .shell__slot--optional { display: none } }`; `src/components/overlays/MenuPopover.jsx` (five items, no Settings).
Evidence: visible controls at 700px: Settings, Notifications, Share, Apps, Chat all 1. At 699, 683 (1366px at 200%), 390 and 320 (1280px at 400%): all 0; the Menu lists Why this matters, How this is calculated, About this data, Demo guide, Give feedback. Assumptions (renewal rate, near-cap threshold) and Reset demo changes live only in Settings. The phone header currently holds only the theme toggle, Menu and avatar.
Expected: functionality available at 320 CSS px (WCAG 1.4.10).
Fix: add "Settings" (and "Share link") to the Menu popover when the gear is hidden, or keep the gear visible in the phone header.

**A-03 (shared, major) The rail Menu is clipped at 200% zoom of a 1440x900 screen and keyboard focus scrolls the shell body.** (D02; vis-01 found the clipping, this adds the keyboard side effect)
Where: `.shell__rail` / `.shell__body` in `src/shell/shell.css` (`overflow: hidden` on the body, none on the rail), viewport 720x450 (also 768x432 = 1536x864 at 200%, 800x450).
Evidence: `.shell__rail-menu` rect top 438, bottom 482 in a 450px viewport (a 12px sliver is clickable). After `focus()` by Tab, `.shell__body.scrollTop` becomes 32 (scrollHeight 409, clientHeight 377): the rail, banner and main slide up 32px and stay there. The Menu holds Evidence, Method, About, Demo guide and Feedback.
Expected: reachable and stable at 200% zoom (1.4.4, 1.4.10).
Fix: `.shell__rail { overflow-y: auto; min-height: 0 }` with the Menu in the flow, or switch to the compact bar when `(max-height: 520px)`.

**A-04 (shared, major) The demo-critical text links and sort buttons are 16px tall, under the 24px target minimum.** (D04)
Where: `.kviz-textlink` ("View clause, page N", "See calculation", "See payments") in `src/charts/charts.css:70`, `.clause-link` in `src/styles/app.css`, `.kx-table th .sort` in `src/ui/kit.css`, the breadcrumb links (DS Breadcrumb), `a.rn-attn__title`, `a.rm-link`. (`a.ov-link` on the Overview is 134x46 and fine.)
Evidence (hit testing, 1440x900, fine pointer): "View clause, page 23" 134x16, "See calculation" 101x16, "See payments" 98x17, clause link on Contract detail 135x16, sort buttons 40 to 144 x 16 (Contracts, Matches, Cap table), breadcrumb "Contracts" 59x16 and "Opportunities" 82x16, `a.rn-attn__title` (Renewals "Needs attention now" titles) 235x22, `a.rm-link` (Roadmap) 296x22. 69 groups under 24px; inline links inside sentences are exempt. For the in-row links the WCAG 2.5.8 spacing exception fails too: the row's stretched link (`::after { inset: 0 }`) covers the 24px circle around them, so another target intersects it. axe does not see `::after` areas, which is why it is silent.
Expected: at least 24x24 (Springboard "24px minimum otherwise"; WCAG 2.5.8 AA), the primary demo link included.
Fix: `.kviz-textlink, .clause-link, .kx-table th .sort { min-height: 24px; display: inline-flex; align-items: center }` (or a transparent `::after` inset of 4px), breadcrumb and card links likewise.

### Minor

**A-05 (shared, minor) Font Awesome private-use glyphs are part of 25 accessible names, including form fields.** (D05)
Where: every DS `Button` with `leftIcon`/`rightIcon`, DS `Input` search glyph, DS `Select` chevron; the `<i class="fa-solid fa-...">` is not `aria-hidden` and Chrome includes its `::before` content in the name.
Evidence (CDP AX names): " Open demo guide" (U+F144), "Open renewal radar " (U+F061), " Give feedback" (U+F4AD), " Export opportunities" (U+F019), " Confirm match , Larchmont..." (U+F00C), " Mark answer as correct", " Back to opportunity", " Previous answer", " Reset demo changes", " Go to overview"; textbox "Search " (U+F002), combobox "Status " and "Sort by " (U+F078), "Search contracts ", "Category ". 25 distinct names across the app. Screen readers may speak the glyph or a Unicode name; the exact behaviour was not tested (no screen reader here).
Expected: decorative icons hidden from assistive technology.
Fix: set `aria-hidden="true"` on every `i[class*="fa-"]` in the DS wrapper in `src/ui/ds.js` (or a one-time MutationObserver in `AppFrame`), since `design-system/` cannot change.

**A-06 (shared, minor) At (pointer: coarse) the kit dialog and drawer buttons are 36px tall and the close X is 42px.** (D06, R70)
Where: `src/styles/app.css:50` applies `min-height: 44px` to `.shell__main` and native `dialog` only; the kit `Dialog` and `Drawer` are `div[role=dialog]` portals, so they miss it.
Evidence (hasTouch + isMobile, 1024x768): Cancel 77x37, Save feedback 128x37, Reset changes 128x37, Close panel 108x36, Close settings 124x36, Close demo guide 149x36, Close dialog 112x36; header X in dialogs and drawers 42x42; shell circle buttons (theme, Share, Notifications, Settings, Menu) 42x42 (`::after inset -6px` on 32px); toast close 32x32; `a.rn-attn__title`, `a.rm-link`, breadcrumb links 16 to 22px tall.
Expected: 44x44 at a coarse pointer (R70).
Fix: add `.kx-dialog, .kx-drawer` to the coarse selector, widen `.kx-hit::after` and `.shell__circle::after` insets by 1px, and give toast close a 44px hit area.

**A-07 (shared, minor) Shell tooltips cannot be dismissed with Escape and vanish when the pointer moves onto them.** (D07, WCAG 1.4.13)
Where: `src/shell/AppShell.jsx` uses the Springboard `Tooltip` for rail, header and Menu buttons; the kit `Tooltip` (`src/ui/Tooltip.jsx`) already does it right.
Evidence: tab to Settings, tooltip "Settings" appears; Escape: still visible, focus unchanged. Hover Roadmap, move the pointer onto the tooltip in 8 steps: the tooltip is gone after 500ms. No `aria-describedby` while visible. Tab away hides it. Tooltips do show on keyboard focus and carry the same text as the `aria-label`.
Expected: dismissible, hoverable, persistent.
Fix: swap the `Tip` wrapper in `AppShell.jsx` for `src/ui/Tooltip.jsx`.

**A-08 (shared, minor) The chart tooltip portal sits outside every landmark (axe `region`).** (D08, known)
Where: `src/charts/Tip.jsx:105` `createPortal(..., document.body)`.
Evidence: 14 of 152 axe runs fail `region`: hero hover or focus (4 nodes: `.kviz-tip__title/__val/__lab/__note`), row hover (10), no-contract tab (8). The tooltip text duplicates the row's `aria-label`.
Fix: `aria-hidden="true"` on the tip (its text is already in the mark's name) or portal into `#shell-main`.

**A-09 (shared, minor) Cap vs spend filter chips show "pressed" by hue only.** (D17, WCAG 1.4.1 and 1.4.11)
Where: `.kx-chip[aria-pressed="true"]` in `src/ui/kit.css:150` (used on Cap vs spend and Supplier matches).
Evidence: pressed border `#1f6feb` vs unpressed `#74757e`: luminance ratio 1.01:1 (dark); `#0969da` vs `#74757e` 1.13:1 (light); the only other difference is a 14% accent tint. Under achromatopsia emulation "All contracts 24" is barely distinguishable (`.scratch/a11y/cvd/crop-spend-chips.png`). The Opportunities chips (`.kviz-chipbtn`) change luminance and pass.
Fix: add a leading check glyph or 2px border plus an opaque tint with a luminance step, as `.kviz-chipbtn` does.

**A-10 (shared, minor) Forced-colors mode loses every "current" and "selected" cue.** (D11)
Where: `src/shell/shell.css` (rail `.is-active` background tint), `src/ui/kit.css` (`.kx-routetab.is-current`, `.kx-chip[aria-pressed]`), `src/charts/charts.css` (only one `forced-colors` block).
Evidence (`forcedColors: active`, `.scratch/a11y/forced/`): all three Cap vs spend tabs get the same 2px underline (transparent border forced to link colour, computed `border-bottom: rgb(255,255,0) 2px` on all three); the active rail item is identical to its neighbours; pressed and unpressed chips match; the coverage meter's matched fill disappears so "84%" reads as an almost empty bar.
Expected: current page and pressed state perceivable (1.4.1, 1.4.11 under forced colours).
Fix: a `@media (forced-colors: active)` block: current tab `border-bottom-color: Highlight` and others `Canvas`, rail `.is-active { outline: 2px solid Highlight }`, chip pressed `border-width: 2px` or `forced-color-adjust: none` fill, meter fill `forced-color-adjust: none`.

**A-11 (shared, minor) Visible labels are not in the accessible name on the phone bottom bar and the avatar.** (D09, WCAG 2.5.3 Level A)
Where: `src/shell/rail.js` (`short: 'Flags'`, `short: 'Renewals'` vs labels "Opportunities", "Renewal radar"), `src/shell/AppShell.jsx` avatar (`aria-label="Account: ..."`, visible "MB").
Evidence: CDP names on a 390px phone: button "Opportunities" shows "Flags" (36 state scans), "Renewal radar" shows "Renewals" (36), "Account: Marchbank commercial team" shows "MB" (76, every page, every width). Voice control "click Flags" or "click MB" fails.
Fix: captions that match the name ("Opportunities", "Renewal radar") or `aria-label` equal to the caption; avatar `aria-label="MB, Account: Marchbank commercial team"`.

**A-12 (shared, minor) The Menu buttons expose `aria-haspopup="menu"` but not `aria-expanded`.** (D10)
Where: `src/shell/AppShell.jsx` (rail Menu and the phone header Menu).
Evidence: `aria-expanded` is absent both closed and open; no `aria-controls`.
Fix: pass `open` into the shell and set `aria-expanded={open}`.

**A-13 (shared, minor) The Overview stacked bar identifies its segments by colour alone, and its order differs from the cards under it.** (D18, WCAG 1.4.1)
Where: `src/charts/StackedBar.jsx` and `HeadlineBreakdown` in `src/charts/Headline.jsx` (`focusable={false}`: the bar is `aria-hidden` and pointer-only).
Evidence: bar order is over cap, price increase, close to cap, renewal; card order is over cap, close to cap, renewals, price increases. The segments carry only a value (when it fits), no name or glyph, and the £0.3m segment has no label. Under achromatopsia the first and last segments are near-identical greys (`.scratch/a11y/cvd/achromatopsia-overview.png`). **A-14 (shared, minor) Light theme: the toolbar search glyph and select chevron are 2.84:1.** (D12, WCAG 1.4.11)

**A-15 (shared, minor) Cumulative spend chart: x-axis month labels overlap at 390px.** (D21)

**A-16 (shared, minor) Toasts: 6 seconds, reached only after 35 Tab presses, empty region always present.** (D16, WCAG 2.2.1)

### Nits
