## What changed against the research kit and chart library

Kit: AppShell, WMark, ThemeToggle, useTheme, CommandPalette and CapMeter are not ported (shell is A1's, `BulletList` replaces CapMeter, no search palette in the requirements). DataTable has the critic 3.6 guards and a keyboard-focusable named scroll region only when it scrolls; Menu, Popover and Tooltip are portals with flip and clamp; Dialog, ConfirmDialog and Drawer share one layer manager (Escape closes only the top layer, so a confirm over a drawer works); Toast has a polite and an assertive live region.

Charts (critic 3.5, 3.7): h4 became h3; `OpportunityList` takes all ranked flags with status, a `format` prop (exact pounds by default), two-line reasons, a type pill, confidence pill, review status pill and an `extra` slot, never re-sorts, reviewed rows muted; `BulletList` shows over-by, cap word, basis and source, two-line titles, and `capTable()` is the "Show table" twin; `CoverageBlock` takes `unmatched` (the engine's £23,830,000), `pending` (£300,000 awaiting review, third bar segment), all suppliers and a note per row; `RadarLanes` takes `extraFor(row)` rendered full width under each row; `RadarStrip` takes `caption`; `HeadlineTile` is split into `HeadlineSentence` (no heading element, for the PageHeader title) and `HeadlineBreakdown`, `footer` slot for the sum line; adapters now take `estate` (`headlineProps(estate)`, `radarRows(estate)`, `capItems(estate)`, `upliftRows(estate)`, `opportunityItems(estate)`, `coverageProps(estate)`, `cumulativeSeries(estate, id)`, `yearBarsProps(estate, id)`); tooltip strings never say "saving" (the opportunity note is `COPY.caveat.chartTooltip`); the DS-colliding name `DataTable` in the chart barrel is now `ChartTable`.

A keyboard-focused chart mark or `Tooltip` keeps its tooltip while the page scrolls to reveal it (the research version closed it, so rows below the fold never showed one on Tab).

## Requests and notes for other owners

The rail Menu: pass the anchor element from `onMenu(el)` to `<Menu anchor={el} placement="right-end">` (see the gallery). Your `app.css` focus rule is not `!important` now: kit and chart rules that reset the outline restore it themselves (checked: 15 controls in both themes).
- **A1 / integrator**: I added `tests/ui-checks.mjs` and `tests/fixtures/ui-gallery.jsx` (new files, outside the ownership table, nothing else touched). Add `node tests/ui-checks.mjs` to `npm test` if wanted. A `"type": "module"` in `package.json` would silence the node warning (also asked by A2).

## Known gaps

- Dialogs and drawers animate in (200ms) but not out; a reduced-motion preference is honoured by A1's global rule.
- Firefox and Safari not run (Chromium 1194 only). The charts use container queries and `color-mix()` (Chrome 111, Safari 16.2, Firefox 113).
- `OpportunityList` clamps a reason to two lines; the longest ones (coverage and low-confidence appends) are cut with an ellipsis in the row and complete in the tooltip, the accessible name and the flag drawer.
- The `BulletList` spend-to-payments drill-down is the view's to wire (button in `extra`); `capItems` carries what it needs (`id`).
- No virtualisation: 24 cap rows and 19 opportunity rows render in a few milliseconds; the payments table is paged.
- `adapters.js` imports wording from `src/lib/copy.js` (A2) so labels match across screens; the rest of `src/charts` imports nothing from the app.
