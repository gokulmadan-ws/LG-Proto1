# Handoff V6: Contracts register and Contract detail

Status: built and verified in a browser. `tests/e2e/V6.mjs` runs 57 checks and passes against my isolated build (`.scratch/V6`, entry `src/dev/V6.jsx`) and against the real app built from `src/main.jsx` (`.scratch/V6real`, with the other views as they stood). `node tests/smoke.mjs --dist .scratch/V6real` passes on `#/contracts` and `#/contracts/C-005` in both themes; its one failure at the time was `#/evidence` at 390px (V7's page, +29px of sideways scroll in main), not mine.

| Route | What you get |
|---|---|

Query parameters on the register (all shareable, survive reload and Back): `q`, `category` (name as written, e.g. `ICT`), `sort` (`id|title|supplier|category|start|end|value|flags`), `dir` (`asc|desc`; each key has its own first direction: numbers high to low, everything else A to Z or oldest first). The detail takes `?flag=<id>` (the global drawer). Clause links use `from=contracts`, so the Source viewer's way back is "Back to contract".

## Numbers and strings

Nothing is derived in the views. Every pound, percentage, date, band, verdict and sentence comes from `useEstate()`, the engine output or `src/lib/copy.js` (`answerFor`, `reasonFor`, `capUsedText`, `capStateLabel`, `upliftResultText`, `latestEndText`, `needsAttentionText`, `noticeDeadlineNote`, `relativeText`, `bandLabel`, `handcheckLine`, `sourceLabel`, `COPY.pages.*`, `COPY.empty.contractsNoMatch`, `COPY.partialCoverageNote`, `COPY.vatNote`, `COPY.paymentsCaption`, `COPY.questionsGroupLabel`, `COPY.buttons.seeCalculation`). The chips and lines use `closeAt = estate.opts.nearCapThreshold`, so the Settings threshold also moves the charts here.

Strings that `copy.js` does not hold, all in `src/views/contracts/strings.js` in the deck's voice (move them into copy.js if you want one home): register (panel title "Contract register", toolbar and field labels, "All categories", "Showing n of 24 contracts", column headers, the hand-check hint and breakdown, the Source rule sentence, the no-filter-match title and body); detail (eyebrow, the not-found title, body and button, the flags panel description and Watch list intro, key-figure labels and feet, the questions panel description, "Check the clause before you rely on this answer.", "View contract value clause, page N", "Checked by hand: correct", the Derived labels and sub-lines, the spend and by-year labels, the payments labels, "After end date", the total and after-end total rows, the payments note). "Latest end date" is the Derived label with "If every extension is used." in the sub-line (the deck's long label split in two so the method link fits beside it).

## Decisions worth knowing

- **Flag count** in the register is the number of ranked flags on the contract (indicative £ above 0, reviewed ones included, so marking a flag Explained does not change the count; the detail page shows it as reviewed). Watch list flags (GBP 0) are on the detail page only. 15 contracts show at least one flag, matching the headline N.
- **Missing maximum** (13 contracts): the answer is "No maximum stated. We used the contract value (£x)." and its link goes to the contract value clause (`X-<id>-awardedTotalValue`, "View contract value clause, page 5"), as the A2 handoff and R49 ask.
- **Hidden columns**: from 700px to 1180px wide the register hides category, route and start so it fits at 1024 without scrolling; the contract page shows them. Phones scroll the whole table inside its region. At 1366 and 1440 all ten columns fit.
- **Derived panel sticks** only when the window is at least 890px tall (the tallest panel, C-004, is 763px). Shorter windows get a normal column.
- **Chart height**: `CumulativeLine` is 340px, and 440px when the line ends between 100% and 135% of the cap (C-007 at 114%), because the library's end label collides with its "Cap" label there (see requests).
- **Matched supplier** column shows the supplier it was matched to and the method and score ("Normalised, 0.98", "Confirmed by you, 0.73"). After you confirm the Larchmont match the 0.73 stays visible, as on the matches tab.
- **Payments** are sorted newest first (Date and Amount are sortable, local state, the page resets on sort). The by-year table shows pence so its total equals the spend figure exactly.
- Not shown, because the engine does not give it: a per-year share of an annual cap.

## Requests for others (the integrator applies)

- **A3, kit.css**: `.kx-table th` sets the header surface (`background: var(--bg-1)`) and it also lands on `tbody th` (a `rowHeader` column), which shows as a grey band in light mode. Patch: `.kx-table tbody th { background: transparent; }`. I worked around it in `Contracts.css` and `ContractDetail.css`; V3 and V5 may see it too.
- **A3, `CumulativeLine.jsx`**: the end-label collision test (`avoid`, threshold 22) only looks at the first line of the two-line end label. When the line ends just above the cap (C-007, 114%) "114% of cap" prints over "Cap £5.4m". A threshold of about 36, or testing the second line too, fixes it. My taller plot for that range can then go.
- **V3, Method page**: "Latest end date" links to `#/method?s=notice` (the section that says the deadline is counted from the end of the current term, not the end if every extension is used) and "Next price review" to `#/method?s=ranking` (the section that defines it). A sentence in `uplift` about the next review date would be a better home for the second.
- **Integrator**: `dist/` still needs `npm run build`. `tests/run-e2e.mjs` picks up `V6.mjs` as it is.

## Known gaps and what I did not verify

- Chromium only. The stretched row link relies on `position: relative` on a table row (fine in current Chromium, Firefox and Safari to my knowledge, but not run).
- On phones the register is the scrolling table, not a card list.
- The Find a Tender variant of the Source chip (icon and label) is built but no sample contract uses it, so it was never on screen.
- Hand-check pills on the detail page were tested with seeded `kontor-handcheck` storage; the buttons that write it live in V3's Source viewer.
- The register Panel title ("Contract register") sits under the h1 "Contracts" by design; if you want a different label, it is `REGISTER.panelTitle`.
