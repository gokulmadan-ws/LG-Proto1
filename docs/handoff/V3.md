## Source viewer

- Layout: breadcrumb (`<origin> / <contract title> / <clause>`, all hash hrefs), a toolbar, then two panes. Left: a paper-like page (max 720px, A4 proportions, running header, clause headings and numbers in Geist Mono, footer) on a stage. Right: the answer panel.
- Highlight: only the block whose `extractionIds` includes the current extraction becomes a `<mark>` (2px accent outline, 14% accent tint, a visible "Cited clause" chip that sits outside the mark so `mark.textContent` equals the quote exactly). On mount the mark is centred in the content area and takes focus (two frames after the router's h1 focus, so it wins on route changes). The first landing also does a short ring pulse (reduced motion removes it).
- Paper surface: token based, no hex. Dark: a lifted `--bg-1` sheet on a `--bg-canvas` stage. Light: a white sheet on a `--bg-2` stage. Both read well; contrast is covered by axe.
- Panel: question (`Question 1 of 9`, label, help), the answer in plain words (`answerFor` from copy.js; a rate card shows as a small table), `ConfidencePill` plus the score, `Not found` pill when the answer is "not found", clause, page (`23 of 70`), document title, the quote as a plain `<blockquote>` (hairline box, no coloured left border), the illustrative label, and the hand-check (`Mark answer as correct` / `Mark answer as incorrect`, `aria-pressed`, saved with `actions.setHandcheck`, toast from copy.js, "n of 336 answers checked by hand").
- Toolbar (pinned while you read on screens 700px and wider): `Back to <origin>`, `Open contract`, `Previous answer`, `Answer 3 of 14`, `Next answer`. Stepping follows the question order (Q1 to Q9, 14 fields), keeps `?from=`, and replaces the history entry (so the browser Back button still leaves the screen in one step). Previous and Next are disabled at the ends. Every button is an outline button: the page has no primary button.
- The panel is sticky only when it fits the window (measured), so no control is ever out of reach. At 1440x900 it is pinned; at 1366x768 it scrolls with the page and the toolbar still pins.
- Missing extraction, an extraction that belongs to another contract, an unknown contract, or a page that is not materialised: the copy deck 7.9 state (`This page isn't in the sample.` as the h1, the body, an `Open contract` button; `Open contracts` when the contract itself is unknown). No crash. The one primary button of the page is the only button in that state.
- Tab title: `usePageTitle(clauseRef)`, so `Clause 14.3 | Kontor financial layer`.

### `?from=`

`opportunities` (default), `overview`, `renewals`, `spend`, `contracts`, `roadmap`, `method`, `evidence`. Each gives the breadcrumb label and the way back: `Back to opportunity`, `Back to overview`, `Back to renewal radar`, `Back to cap vs spend`, `Back to contract` (goes to `#/contracts/<id>`), `Back to roadmap`, `Back to method page`, `Back to evidence`. Any other value falls back to opportunities. The label is `Back to opportunity` only for the opportunities origin; I did not want "opportunity" on a button that returns to the contract register or the method page.

## Method page

- Sticky section list on the left (a button that folds it away below 1180px), twelve sections, ids exactly `as-of, notice, radar, spend, matching, cap, uplift, indicative, ranking, confidence, data, limits`. **The id sits on the section heading** (`<h2 id="cap" tabIndex={-1}>`), so `#/method?s=cap` scrolls to and focuses that heading (`document.activeElement.id === 'cap'`). The router moves focus to the h1 on a route change, so the heading takes focus two frames later. The list marks the section being read with `aria-current="location"`.
- Section titles and lead sentences come from `COPY.method` (copy.js). Extra prose is mine, in plain short paragraphs.
- Constants: every value comes from `DEFAULTS` (engine.js). The tables show Default and "In use now" (`estate.opts`), with "Changed in Settings" when they differ. Each value sits in `<span data-const="<key>" data-kind="default|inuse" data-raw="<raw>">` so tests can compare them. Covered: `renewalRate`, `nearCapThreshold`, `upliftTolerancePp`, `upliftMinGBP`, `autoAcceptScore`, `suggestScore`.
- Worked examples are live (they follow your Settings, triage and match decisions): notice deadlines for C-001, C-017, C-018; radar bands with the computed boundary dates (`estate.radar.boundaries`) and the contracts in each band; spend after the end date (C-007); coverage; four payee names (the Kestrelvale normalised and alias matches, the Larchmont suggestion, Mirefield unmatched); cap used for C-005 (£8,350,000 against £5,000,000, 167.0%, £3,350,000), C-011 (annual cap by contract year), C-001 (close to cap projection, £642,478), and the C-003 "At least" note; price increase check for C-004 (£188,200) and the C-003 near miss, plus the reasons the check cannot run; renewal for C-002 (£145,000); the first five ranked flags; C-018 low confidence. Clause links use `from="method"`.
- Notes required by the brief: VAT note and data-window note (the `data` section lead is the copy.js text), "5% is a prototype assumption" with the Sheffield and Sefton rationale (no £1.7m figure, per decision 7; links to the two LGA sources with "(opens in a new tab)" and to `#/evidence`), the headline sum line, "The headline is about 4% of the £153.5m in the payment files", what the prototype does not do (no ingestion, no cross-council comparison, no invoices, no cause of a payment, no PO matching). No inputs or calculators. The one button is `Open settings` (outline), which calls `useUI().openSettings`.

## Known gaps and honest notes

- The severity cut-off for a price increase (£100,000) and the confidence bands (0.90 and 0.75) are typed on the Method page. They are literals inside `engine.js` and `copy.js`, not in `DEFAULTS`. The e2e does not guard them; see the request below. The over-cap threshold (100%) is the definition of a cap and is also typed.
- The "about 4%" sentence divides `totals.totalGBP` by `coverage.totalGBP` in the view. `copy.js` has `headlineShareText`, which says "equivalent to 4% of the £153.5m in the sample payment files"; I wrote "about 4% of the £153.5m in the payment files" to match the brief word for word.
- Tables on the Method page scroll sideways inside their own box on phones (they become keyboard-focusable named regions, which the kit `DataTable` handles). I did not redesign them as stacked cards.
- Firefox and Safari were not run (Chromium only). The paper uses `aspect-ratio`, `color-mix` and `text-wrap`.
- At 1366x768 the answer panel is taller than the window and scrolls with the page (the toolbar still pins). The question label can be scrolled out of view on arrival; the answer, clause, page, document, quote and hand-check stay visible.
- Real contract text is not shown anywhere; the viewer renders the illustrative page text from the dataset and says so.

## Requests for others (integrator)

1. `src/ui/kit.css`: `.kx-table-wrap` should be `position: relative`. An `.sr-only` span inside a scrolling table (the `ClauseLink` context text) is `position: absolute`, escapes the wrapper's clipping, and widened the whole document by 1px to 107px on a table with a clause link. I worked around it in `Method.css` (`.mth-section .kx-table-wrap { position: relative }`); any other view that puts a `ClauseLink` with `context` inside a `DataTable` will hit it.
2. `src/routes.js`: `from=method` highlights Opportunities in the rail (an unknown `from` falls back). If you want the Method page to highlight nothing, add `method` handling there.
3. `src/lib/engine.js` / `copy.js` (A2): export the severity cut-off and the confidence thresholds so the Method page can read them instead of typing them; optionally export a `headlineShare(totals, coverage)` so the view does not divide.
