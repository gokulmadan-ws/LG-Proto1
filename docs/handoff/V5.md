### Query parameters (shareable, replace history so Back is not polluted)

- `#/spend?state=over|close|within` filter chip. `#/spend?payments=<contractId>` opens the payments drawer (unknown id opens nothing).
- `#/spend/matches?status=review|accepted|unmatched|yours`.
- `#/spend/no-contract?payee=<name as paid>` opens the payee drawer.

### Payments drawer (kit `Drawer`, `size="lg"`)

Four figures (spend, cap with source and basis, share with the state pill, over by or headroom, all marked indicative), the partial-coverage note, then payments with columns Date, Reference, Name as paid, Amount (my own `PaymentList`, not the kit `PaymentsTable`, so the columns are the AC's), paged 12 at a time with a subtotal row that covers every payment, not just the page. ## Behaviour worth knowing

- **Reject adds the payee to the no-contract list.** The engine buckets a rejected payee with the no-contract spend, so after Reject the tab says 9 payees, £24,130,000 and lists Larchmont Grounds Maintenance; Undo decision returns it to Suggested and the list to 8 payees, £23,830,000. The headline, caps and watch list return to the starting numbers exactly. The toast is the deck's "Match rejected. {name} is now unmatched."
- Confirm toast is built from the NEW estate (`computeEstate` with the pending decision), so it names the right contract: "Match confirmed. 6 payments (£300,000) now count towards Grounds maintenance."
- After a decision, focus moves to the row's new button (Confirm becomes Undo, and back); if the row left the filtered list, focus goes to the active chip.
- The summary tiles and the total over cap come from `estate.capRows` (engine states), so they follow Settings (near-cap 80% gives 3/4/17, 90% gives 3/2/19) and ignore review status (marking C-005 Explained does not change them).
- Annual caps: the row's "Spend to date" figure is the worst contract year (£512,000 of £450,000 annual cap), as the engine defines it; the drawer says so.
- The BulletList is fed `closeAt={estate.opts.nearCapThreshold}`.
