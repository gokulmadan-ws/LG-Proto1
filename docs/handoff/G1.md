Status: built and verified in a full-app build (`.scratch/G1full`) and a dev-entry build (`.scratch/G1`, `src/dev/G1.jsx`). `KONTOR_DIST=.scratch/G1full node tests/run-e2e.mjs G1` passes 40 of 40 checks. `node tests/smoke.mjs --dist .scratch/G1full` passes 117 of 117. `tests/e2e/V7.mjs` (Menu) and `tests/e2e/V1.mjs` still pass against the same build. ## What exists

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| `#/guide` | One h1 "Guide" (title `Guide | Kontor financial layer`, as at line, one-line intro). Nine sections, ids `start tour screens try numbers real glossary keys faq`; the id is on the section `<h2 tabIndex=-1>`, so `#/guide?s=<id>` scrolls to and focuses it (same pattern as `Method.jsx`, first landing instant, in-page clicks glide). ### Sections

1. **Start here**: the two-sentence lead, a sample-data note (with a link that opens About this data), four live tiles (contracts, payments, answers, contracts flagged), three numbered steps, the ONE primary button "Open the overview" and a link to the tour.
2. **The five-minute tour**: a live-numbers note with a Pill ("Showing the starting numbers" or "Showing your changes"), then four step cards (big numeral, route chip, Do, Say, "You should see", "Open step" link; step 3 also has the real `ClauseLink`). Step 1 shows the headline sentence and the exact sum line plus "Starting value" when your changes moved it; step 2 "3 contracts need attention now" and the next-3-months line; step 3 "£8.4m paid against a £5.0m maximum" and "Clause 14.3, page 23 of 70"; step 4 the close line and the spoken variant.
3. **Screens at a glance**: nine cards (Overview, Opportunities, Renewal radar, Cap vs spend with its three tab links, Contracts and contract detail, Source viewer, How this is calculated, Roadmap, Why this matters), each with the rail icon, what it answers, who uses it, and links.
4. **Try these**: seven rows with a live "You should see": switch theme (a real button), mark the top over-cap flag Explained, confirm the Larchmont match, change the renewal rate (button opens Settings), check an answer by hand, share a link, reset (a real button that runs the same destructive confirm as Settings; with nothing to reset it says so in a toast).
5. **How to read the numbers**: a warning callout (the caveat, verbatim from `COPY.caveat.long`), an info callout ("What indicative means", `COPY.caveat.tooltip`), the four bases as cards with live value, count and basis and the exact sum line under them, confidence (the three pills), notice window, over cap and close to cap (the three cap pills plus "Above contract value (estimate)", live counts).
6. **What is real and what is not**: three columns (Real, Made up for the demo, Not built yet) with links to the method page, evidence page and roadmap.
7. **Words you will see**: a definition list of 14 terms, each one or two sentences.
8. **Keyboard and links**: moving around (with `Kbd`), ten shareable address patterns with an example link each, and what the back button does (checked: filters and panels opened from a list use replace, links push).
9. **Questions people ask**: six native `<details>` (headline, "At least", confirm a match, own data, Needs review, not covered). The own-data answer says what is and is not done today and promises no ingestion.

## Known gaps and notes for others

- The review workflow's scripts (they lived in `tests/review/` while I worked, and were gone when I finished) asserted the Menu has exactly five items (`fun-robust.mjs`, `fun-links.mjs`, `fun-known.mjs`). Any copy of them needs `Guide` after `About this data`. I did not touch them.
- Under 700px the header tabs are hidden (existing behaviour), so the Guide tab is not visible there; the Guide is reached through the Menu, the Demo guide link, or a shared link. On a phone the Kontor app tab reads as idle while you are on the Guide.
- Step 4 of the tour links to the Overview (the close panel is at its foot); there is no address for that panel.
- Links from the Guide into the source viewer use `from=opportunities`, so the viewer's way back reads "Back to opportunity". The viewer has no `from=guide` (not mine to change).
- The Supplier matches tab removes `?status=review` (it is the default), so the Guide links to the plain tab.
- The Guide runs four extra engine passes on each change (the starting numbers and one "what if" each for Explained, the match and the rate), about 25 ms in all. It is memoised on the estate and state objects only.
- The phone contents select is the DS `Select`; its border is the DS default.
- Chromium only (container queries, `color-mix`, `details`).
