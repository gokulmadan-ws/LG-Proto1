Action tracker

Today the prototype finds the money. This feature helps the council chase it, and shows what came back.

The idea in one picture

An opportunity marked "Under review" becomes a card that someone owns, works on and closes.

  4. 4Work itOwner, due date, next steps, who to contact, draft email.

Under review

Counted in the headline. Stage: Checking internally.

  1. 1
    Who and whenStage, owner and due date. Due starts as the action-by date (for example a notice date), or 14 days after it starts.
  2. 2
    Next stepsA short checklist for each flag type. It turns "Reasons this may not be a saving" into things to do.
  3. 3
    Who to contactThe contract names both sides' representatives, so Kontor can pull them out with a clause link, like every other answer. Council roles come from a sample staff list.
  4. 4
    Draft emailFilled from the contract facts. Pick a person and the email changes. You copy it and send it yourself. The app sends nothing.
  5. 5
    Exit termsFacts that Kontor already extracts. The Surrey team asks about termination, so this is cheap to show. There is no options maths.
  6. 6
    Activity logAn audit trail (a record of who did what, and when). This keeps a human in charge, which councils want. Later it can teach the system what usually works.

What the build needs

Plain view. The detailed version comes in the Markdown spec.

New sample data

  • A staff list. About 8 fictional council people with roles and departments.
  • Named representatives. A new extracted answer for each contract: both sides' contacts, with clause and page.
  • Step lists and email templates for each flag type: over cap, close to cap, renewal and price increase.
  • Example history. 5 closed actions from May to September 2026, on contracts with no flag today.

New saved state

  • One record for each action: stage, owner, due date, steps ticked, notes, log, outcome, cash recovered and cost avoided.
  • Kept in the browser, like the review status today. Each viewer has their own board.
  • Four example cards come pre-loaded, all open. Reset puts them back.

Rules that stay true

  • £6.1m at the start. The example cards are all open, and the example history is on contracts with no flag, so the demo headline does not move.
  • No new claims. "Resolved" amounts are the user's own figures, labelled that way.
  • Works offline. No server, no AI calls, no email sent.

The demo in 90 seconds

Follows the current 5-minute tour, after "Opportunities". On Home before that, hover a renewal diamond to show which contract it is.

  1. Start itOpen Highways (£3.35m over cap). Set "Under review". Now someone owns it.
  2. See the boardClick "Open in Actions". The card is in To start, next to four others already in progress.
  3. Know who to callOpen the card. Set Dana Okafor as owner. Show the representatives, pulled from clause 3 of the contract.
  4. Make the first moveCopy the draft email. Ten seconds, and the contract facts are already in it. Mention that templates could be swapped for AI drafts later.
  5. Close the loopDrag Street lighting to Closed. Choose Resolved and enter £150,000 cash recovered, the credit the supplier agreed.
  6. Show what came backOpen Results. Value secured goes from £203,100 to £353,100. This is the number your finance director asks for.

Added in draft 2

D7What do we call the money that came back?

  • Value secured = Cash recovered + Cost avoided
  • Savings = Revenue gained + Cost savings

The prototype only says "saving" on the Roadmap, because flagged amounts can shrink when tested. "Value secured" keeps that rule and still reads as money won.

D8Pre-load example history in Results?

  • Yes: 5 closed actions, May to September 2026
  • No. Results starts empty

One row looks thin. The history is on contracts with no flag today, so the headline stays at £6.1m.

D9What does clicking a renewal diamond do?

  • Open that renewal in the side drawer
  • Go to the Renewal radar page

One click from Home to the clause and the Action tab. The "Open renewal radar" button already does the other.

Not in this build

  • Sending email or invites. You copy the text. The app sends nothing.
  • Logins, teams or permissions. Owners come from a sample staff list.
  • Reminders or notifications. Due dates show on the board only.
  • AI recommendations. A Roadmap row only. They need a history of closed actions first.
  • Options analysis. No exit cost against overspend maths. Dropped after the 12 Oct sync.
  • A shared board. Each browser keeps its own board, like the rest of the prototype.
  • Checking Value secured. The figures are what the team types in.
  • A new home for the evidence cases. They stay where they are, in the Menu.
